# Governance

## Project Structure

OSCD is a community-driven project with a simple governance model.

## Roles

### Coordinator

- **Who:** Project founder (Sil / amurlaniakea)
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Strategic direction of the project
  - Final decision-making authority
  - Invitation of Guardians
  - Conflict resolution
- **Accountability:** The Coordinator is accountable to the community through transparent decision-making documented in this repository

### Guardian

- **Who:** Trusted community members invited by the Coordinator
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Respond to incident reports
  - Help resolve active cases
  - Document resolved cases
  - Mentor new contributors
- **Requirements:**
  - Demonstrated expertise in security, code audit, or incident response
  - At least 3 meaningful contributions to the project
  - Commitment to privacy and ethical conduct
- **Accountability:** Guardians who violate privacy or ethical standards are removed immediately

### Contributor

- **Who:** Anyone who submits a PR, opens an Issue, or helps in discussions
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Follow the Code of Conduct
  - Respect privacy of victims
  - Maintain quality standards
- **Privileges:**
  - Participate in discussions
  - Propose changes via PRs
  - Document case studies

### Reporter

- **Who:** Any developer who submits an incident report
- **Rights:**
  - Privacy and confidentiality
  - Respectful treatment
  - Transparency about how their data is used
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Provide accurate information
  - Consent to anonymized documentation (optional)

## Decision Making

### Routine Decisions
- Made by any Contributor via PR (documentation, guides, case studies)
- Merged by the Coordinator or a Guardian

### Significant Decisions
- New partnerships or collaborations
- Changes to governance or privacy policy
- Addition of new Guardians
- Made by the Coordinator after community discussion (minimum 7 day comment period)

### Emergency Decisions
- Active attacks requiring immediate coordination
- Privacy violations
- Made by the Coordinator, retroactively documented and reviewed

## Transparency

- All governance decisions are documented in this file or in GitHub Issues
- Case statistics are public (number of cases, types, resolution status)
- No private decision-making that affects the community

## Amendments

This governance document can be amended by:

1. Opening a GitHub Issue proposing the change
7-day community comment period
3. Coordinator approval
4. Documentation of the change
